# School Board Weighs Campus Camera Network

*By Maya Chen, Ball Street Journal*

The Harbor Point school board voted 4 to 3 on Monday to study a network of cameras for the district's four campuses, with a final decision due before summer.

The proposal would place cameras at building entrances, parking lots and the stadium, and would keep recordings for 14 days. Board member Ellis Grant said the aim is to shorten the time it takes staff to review an incident, not to watch students in class.

The three-year cost is about $1.2 million, including storage. The superintendent said the district would not use face matching and would not share footage with outside agencies unless required by a warrant.

Parents at Tuesday's meeting split. Some asked for cameras after two after-school fights this fall. Others said a camera on every door would change how children move through the day.

The board will hold a work session next month. If approved, installation would start at the high school and then move to the elementary campuses.
