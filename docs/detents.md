# Dial & slider wording — editable reference

How to use this: edit only the **text after the colon** on each line. Keep the
`n (value):` prefix and the pole headers exactly as they are so I can map your
edits straight back into the code. Send it back when you're done and I'll apply it.

- **MEANING** = the plain explanation shown in the controller walkthrough (the info "i" slides).
- **1–5** = the five detents, left end (1) to right end (5). `(value)` is the stored 0–100 position.

---

## RADIO (the station dials)

### ENERGY — LOW ↔ HIGH
MEANING: How much energy the track carries — from near-silent ambience to relentless, driving intensity.
1 (0): Comatose — basically elevator music
2 (25): Chill, lo-fi homework vibes
3 (50): A steady head-nod
4 (75): Hyped, pre-game energy
5 (100): Unhinged — the gym-bro anthem

### MOOD — BAD ↔ GOOD
MEANING: The emotional tone the music pushes you toward, from bleak and heavy to euphoric feel-good.
1 (0): Villain-origin-story sad
2 (25): Rainy-day melancholy
3 (50): Emotionally neutral, like a hold tone
4 (75): Good mood, main-character energy
5 (100): Euphoric — serotonin maxed out

### TEXTURE — SMOOTH ↔ ROUGH
MEANING: How the sound is produced — glassy and polished at one end, distorted and lo-fi at the other.
1 (0): Buttery — spa-playlist clean
2 (25): Smooth, lightly polished
3 (50): A little fuzz and texture
4 (75): Gritty, basement-recording raw
5 (100): Blown out — your speakers are scared

## VIDEOS (the reel dials) — grouped by vibe

### COMEDY

#### SAFE ↔ EDGY
MEANING: How far the jokes go — wholesome crowd-pleasers vs content that's offensive on purpose.
1 (0): Grandma-approved, zero risk
2 (25): Mild, office-party safe
3 (50): A little spicy
4 (75): Edgy — toeing the line
5 (100): Cancelable — there is no line left

#### MAINSTREAM ↔ OBSCURE
MEANING: Whether the feed serves what everyone has already seen or deep cuts almost no one has.
1 (0): The #1 clip everyone already sent you
2 (25): Popular, widely shared
3 (50): A few deep cuts mixed in
4 (75): Off the beaten feed
5 (100): So underground it has 12 views

#### SINCERE ↔ IRONIC
MEANING: Whether it means what it says or keeps everything at an ironic, winking distance.
1 (0): Earnest — means every single word
2 (25): Mostly sincere
3 (50): Half-joking, you decide
4 (75): Ironic, permanently winking
5 (100): So ironic no one knows what it believes

### LIFESTYLE

#### FREE ↔ PRICEY
MEANING: The price tag of the lifestyle shown — thrifted and free vs luxury flex.
1 (0): Thrifted, free, zero dollars spent
2 (25): Budget — treat-yourself-on-sale
3 (50): Mid-range and reasonable
4 (75): Splurge — treating yourself loudly
5 (100): Rent-money flex, fiscally reckless

#### RAW ↔ POLISHED
MEANING: How produced it looks — unfiltered phone footage vs glossy, ad-perfect production.
1 (0): Filmed on a potato, zero edits
2 (25): Raw and casual
3 (50): Lightly edited
4 (75): Polished, actually produced
5 (100): Glossy — suspiciously ad-perfect

#### BROAD ↔ NICHE   (also used by BODY — edit once, applies to both)
MEANING: Who it is made for — universal appeal vs a hyper-specific inside world.
1 (0): For literally everyone alive
2 (25): Wide appeal
3 (50): A mixed crowd
4 (75): For a specific little crowd
5 (100): Niche final boss — no one knows what you mean

### BODY

#### AESTHETIC ↔ PERFORMANCE
MEANING: What the body content is really selling. AESTHETIC is how it looks on camera — the image, the mirror. PERFORMANCE is what the body can actually do — the results. The feed decides which one it shows you.
1 (0): Pure thirst trap — all mirror, no gym
2 (25): Mostly about the look
3 (50): Looks and gains, both
4 (75): Mostly about what the body can do
5 (100): Pure athlete — results, no posing

#### GENTLE ↔ INTENSE
MEANING: How hard the content pushes you — soft and low-impact vs brutal, maximum-effort.
1 (0): Barely moving — a gentle stretch
2 (25): Easy, low-impact
3 (50): A real push
4 (75): Hard — you'll feel it tomorrow
5 (100): Brutal — leaves you on the floor

### MIND

MEANING: Whether it winds you down or winds you up — calm self-care vs adrenaline and urgency.
1 (0): Horizontal — asleep by minute two
2 (25): Soothing, settling
3 (50): Neutral, just vibing
4 (75): Wakes you up, lights on
5 (100): Full adrenaline — fix-your-life-now

#### EVIDENCE ↔ BELIEF
MEANING: What it runs on — sourced facts and proof vs pure conviction and faith.
1 (0): Cited, sourced, peer-reviewed
2 (25): Evidence-led
3 (50): Half facts, half faith
4 (75): Running on belief
5 (100): Pure vibes — "trust me bro"

#### SINCERE ↔ PERFORMATIVE
MEANING: Whether the selfhood on display is genuinely felt or performed for an audience.
1 (0): Genuinely felt, no audience
2 (25): Mostly sincere
3 (50): A little staged
4 (75): Performing for the camera
5 (100): All show — the camera is the whole point

### MONEY

#### SAFE ↔ RISKY
MEANING: The risk appetite of the money content — guaranteed and boring vs an all-in gamble.
1 (0): Savings-account safe, deeply boring
2 (25): Cautious, low stakes
3 (50): Measured risk
4 (75): High-risk, sweaty-palms
5 (100): All-in on a coin named after a dog

#### SLOW ↔ FAST
MEANING: The time horizon — patient long-game wealth vs get-rich-overnight.
1 (0): Retire-in-40-years patient
2 (25): Slow and steady
3 (50): Mid-pace
4 (75): Quick returns
5 (100): Rich by Friday or bust

#### NEED ↔ STATUS
MEANING: What the spending is for — necessity and getting by vs the flex of being seen.
1 (0): Pure survival — rent and ramen
2 (25): Practical needs
3 (50): A little comfort
4 (75): For the flex
5 (100): Pure clout — bought to be seen

### CULTURE

#### TECH ↔ HUMANITIES
MEANING: Which side of the culture it sits on — code and systems vs people, art and meaning.
1 (0): All code, zero feelings
2 (25): Tech-leaning
3 (50): Where nerds meet poets
4 (75): People, art, big feelings
5 (100): All art and meaning, zero machines

#### CALM ↔ HEATED
MEANING: The temperature of the discourse — level-headed vs an all-out flame war.
1 (0): Zen — nothing rattles it
2 (25): Calm, measured
3 (50): Mild friction
4 (75): Heated, caps-lock energy
5 (100): Comment-section warfare

#### CONSENSUS ↔ HETERODOX
MEANING: Whether it echoes the agreed-upon take or rejects the consensus entirely.
1 (0): The approved, agreed-on take
2 (25): Mainstream consensus
3 (50): A couple contrarian notes
4 (75): Against the grain
5 (100): Devil's advocate nobody asked for
